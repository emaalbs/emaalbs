About EMAAL
Building, scaling, and enabling businesses across Iraq and the region.
Who We Are
EMAAL is a business platform builder and investment-driven group that develops, scales, and invests in ventures across Iraq and the region.
We operate across the full business lifecycle building companies from the ground up, accelerating emerging ventures, and supporting established companies in expanding their market presence.
Through our platforms, ecosystem, and strategic positioning, EMAAL connects private sector leaders with government institutions enabling access, partnerships, and real opportunities.
What We Do
Venture Building & Investment : We build and invest in businesses across high-potential sectors.
Platforms & Ecosystems : We create platforms such as the Iraq Business Summit (IBS).
Government Relations & Market Access : We facilitate connections between private sector and government.
Infrastructure & Innovation : We support projects across digital infrastructure and logistics.
International Engagement & Conferences
EMAAL facilitates participation in leading international conferences and business platforms both within Iraq and globally.
EMAAL supports companies, investors, startups, nonprofit organizations, and public institutions in positioning, coordination, and strategic engagement, enabling participation in well-recognized international conferences and business platforms, while connecting them with relevant stakeholders, markets, and opportunities.
