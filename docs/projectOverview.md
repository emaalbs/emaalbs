## 1. What is EMAAL?

EMAAL Business Space is the **first institutional alliance** working to develop and accelerate businesses, established in **Iraq** and the **United Arab Emirates**. It is not a traditional consultancy — it is a comprehensive business ecosystem that bridges the public and private sectors.

### Core Companies within EMAAL:

- **Al-Nahrain Company** — A multidisciplinary consulting and service company.
- **IFPMC Center** — A research center specializing in economic policies and studies.

### Partnership Sectors (6 Vital Iraqi Economic Sectors):
1. Telecommunications and Technology
2. Oil and Energy
3. Transportation and Supply Chains
4. Banking and Financial Services
5. Insurance and Healthcare
6. Investment and Project Development

### What EMAAL Does (Services):

- **Entrepreneurship** — Supporting individuals and businesses in launching new ventures with guidance, strategies, and resources.
- **Organizing Conferences** — Planning and executing conferences for knowledge exchange, networking, and collaboration (flagship: Iraq Business Summit).
- **Media and Advertising** — Content creation and distribution through Iraq 24 Channel and Iraq 24 Platform.
- **Website Development** — Custom websites that are fast, user-friendly, and SEO-supported.
- **Branding** — Custom logos, visuals, and messaging that connect with audiences.
- **Training and Development** — Educational programs and workshops to improve skills and performance.
- **Commercial Services** — Consulting, market research, and operations management.
- **Business Incubator** — Full incubation program: Application → Onboarding → Development → Resources → Networking → Monitoring → Graduation.

## 4. Brand Identity & Design System

> **Core Identity:** "Strategic Catalyst" (المحفّز الاستراتيجي)
> **Voice:** Authoritative, Visionary, Institutional, Growth-Oriented
> **Vibe:** The UI must feel like entering a summit boardroom — commanding but welcoming.

### 4.1 Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|:-----|:-----|:----|:------|
| **Canvas** | Deep Navy | `#01334D` | Primary background, headers, authority |
| **Canvas/Dark** | Navy Dark | `#011E2F` | Footer, darker sections |
| **Surface** | Card White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards (light mode), modals |
| **Surface/Dark** | Card Glass | `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)` | Cards on dark backgrounds |
| **Primary CTA** | Strategic Gold | `#EEC13B` | Primary CTAs, highlights, badges |
| **Primary Hover** | Deep Gold | `#AB820B` | Hover states, active gold elements |
| **Primary Tint** | Gold Light | `#FFF8E1` | Gold tinted backgrounds |
| **Accent** | Bright Teal | `#006666` | Secondary actions, links, icons |
| **Accent Tint** | Teal Light | `#E0F2F1` | Teal tinted backgrounds |
| **Text/Dark BG** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Headings on dark backgrounds |
| **Text/Dark BG 2** | Soft Silver | `#B0BEC5` | Body text on dark backgrounds |
| **Text/Light BG** | Near Black | `#0A1628` | Headings on light backgrounds |
| **Text/Light BG 2** | Slate | `#546E7A` | Body text on light backgrounds |
| **Light BG** | Warm White | `#F8F9FA` | Light mode page backgrounds |
| **Border/Dark** | — | `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` | Borders on dark backgrounds |
| **Border/Light** | — | `#E0E0E0` | Borders on light backgrounds |

**Shadows:**
- Subtle: `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` — inputs
- Card: `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)` — standard cards
- Elevated: `0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.20)` — modals, featured cards
- Gold Glow: `0 4px 24px rgba(238,193,59,0.30)` — gold CTA hover
- Teal Glow: `0 4px 24px rgba(0,102,102,0.25)` — teal CTA hover

**Gradients:**
- Navy Fade: `linear-gradient(180deg, #01334D 0%, #011E2F 100%)` — hero sections
- Gold Accent: `linear-gradient(135deg, #EEC13B 0%, #AB820B 100%)` — premium badges
- Dark Overlay: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(1,51,77,0.9) 0%, rgba(1,30,47,0.95) 100%)` — image overlays

### 4.2 Typography

| Level | Size | Weight | Font | Usage |
|:------|:-----|:-------|:-----|:------|
| Display | 48-64px | Bold (700) | Montserrat / Cairo | Hero titles, summit banners |
| H1 | 36-42px | Bold (700) | Montserrat / Cairo | Page titles |
| H2 | 28-32px | SemiBold (600) | Montserrat / Cairo | Section headers |
| H3 | 22-24px | SemiBold (600) | Montserrat / Cairo | Card titles |
| H4 | 18-20px | Medium (500) | Montserrat / Cairo | Sub-headers |
| Body Large | 18px | Regular (400) | Montserrat / Cairo | Lead paragraphs |
| Body Base | 16px | Regular (400) | Montserrat / Cairo | Standard text |
| Body Small | 14px | Regular (400) | Montserrat / Cairo | Supporting text |
| Caption | 12px | Medium (500) | Montserrat / Cairo | Labels, tags |
| Overline | 11-12px | Bold (700) | Montserrat | Section labels, UPPERCASE |

- **English:** Montserrat — authority, geometric, modern
- **Arabic:** Cairo — screen-optimized, geometric Arabic
- **Numbers/Data:** Inter — tabular numbers, precision
- **Stats:** Inter Bold, 48-64px, Gold `#EEC13B` for emphasis
- **Heading pattern:** `[OVERLINE gold, uppercase, 0.15em spacing]` → `[HEADING bold, 28-36px]` → `[SUBTITLE regular, 16-18px, muted]`
- **Line Height:** Body 160%, Headings 120-130%
- **Letter Spacing:** Overline `0.1em`, Display headings `-0.02em`

### 4.3 Layout & Spacing

- **Max content width:** 1200px, centered
- **Grid:** 12-column system
- **Section padding:** 32-48px between sections, 24px between cards
- **Border radius:** `12px` for cards, `8px` for inputs, `24px` for pills/badges
- **Buttons:** `rounded-lg` (12px) — professional, not playful
- **Icons:** Sharp geometric line icons, 1.5px stroke, 48px size

### 4.4 Component Specs

**Buttons:**
- Primary CTA: `#EEC13B` background, `#01334D` text, `12px` radius, gold glow on hover → deepens to `#AB820B`
- Secondary: `border-2 border-[#006666]` with teal text
- CTA text on gold buttons: `#01334D` Bold

**Cards (Dark Mode):** `bg-white/5 rounded-xl border border-white/10 backdrop-blur-sm`
**Cards (Light Mode):** `#FFFFFF` with soft shadows, 12px radius

**Hero Sections:** Full-width, real event photography + navy overlay gradient, headline Montserrat Bold 48-64px white, subtitle 18px `#B0BEC5`, gold CTA below

**Statistics Bar:** Count-up animated numbers in gold, labels in muted white — e.g. "45+ Clients" | "6 Sectors" | "3 Companies"

**Footer:** `#011E2F` background, structured columns, gold social icons

### 4.5 Interaction Model

**User Journey:** Discover (اكتشف) → Engage (تفاعل) → Connect (تواصل)

**Navigation:**
- Sticky header on scroll, CTA always visible ("Get Started" in gold)
- Mega-menu for deep sub-nav (IBS, Portfolio sections)
- Language toggle (EN/AR) in header
- 2 clicks max to any content

**Interaction Patterns:**
| Element | Feedback |
|:--------|:---------|
| Gold CTA | Darkens to `#AB820B`, subtle gold glow |
| Service Card | Subtle elevation + border glow on hover |
| Nav Link | Underline animation, color shift |
| Stat Counter | Count-up animation on scroll (0 → 45+) |
| Gallery Image | Lightbox with smooth scale transition |
| Form Submit | Button loading state → success toast |

**Section Transitions:** Content fades in with subtle upward motion on scroll. Gold line/gradient dividers between sections.

**Mobile:** Hamburger menu, full-screen overlay, 48px min touch targets, horizontal swipe for cards/gallery.

### 4.6 Logo & Brand Mark

- **Primary:** "EMAAL" in Montserrat Bold + "BUSINESS SPACE" in Montserrat Medium, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`
- **Arabic:** "إعمال" in Cairo Bold
- **On Dark Navy:** White wordmark + Gold accent
- **On White/Light:** Navy wordmark + Gold accent
- **Favicon:** "E" lettermark, Gold on Navy, 12px rounded
- **IBS Sub-brand:** Same type system, can use more prominent teal, always endorsed "by EMAAL"
- **Min width:** 80px digital, 25mm print
- **Clear space:** Height of the "E" on all sides
- **Never:** Stretch, recolor, add effects, crop, place on busy backgrounds without overlay

### 4.7 Design Anti-Patterns (Kill List)

**Visual — BANNED:**
- ❌ Generic light blue (`#4A90D9`) → use Deep Navy
- ❌ Pure white background (`#FFFFFF` as page bg) → use Warm White `#F8F9FA`
- ❌ Stock photos of handshakes → use real EMAAL event photography
- ❌ Flat zero-depth design → use subtle glass + elevation
- ❌ Overly rounded corners (`rounded-full`) → use `12px` professional radius
- ❌ Rainbow color schemes → Navy + Gold + Teal only

**Content — BANNED:**
- ❌ Buzzword soup ("synergistic paradigm-shifting") → clear, direct language
- ❌ Unnamed achievements ("many events") → specific ("45+ clients, 6 sectors, IBS 2025")
- ❌ Walls of text → use overline + heading + subtitle pattern
- ❌ Missing CTAs → every section ends with a next step
- ❌ Hidden contact info → CTA in header, full details in footer

**Typography — BANNED:**
- ❌ Decorative/script fonts → Montserrat + Cairo only
- ❌ Tiny text for key info → headlines 28px+, body 16px+
- ❌ Center-aligned body text → left-align (right for Arabic)

**Motion — BANNED:**
- ❌ Flashy bouncing/spinning/pulsing → subtle fade-in, slide-up (200-400ms, ease-out)
- ❌ Auto-playing carousels → user-controlled or single hero
- ❌ Parallax overuse → hero only, body content stays static

### 4.8 Target Personas (Design Validation)

Every screen must work for all four:

1. **Dr. Ahmad — Corporate Executive (40-55):** Needs all services visible at a glance, credibility signals (stats, summit photos), one-click contact, professional tone
2. **Noor — Young Entrepreneur (22-35):** Needs clear incubator steps, modern/polished design, mobile-optimized, startup-welcoming messaging
3. **Sarah — International Investor (35-50):** Needs detailed IBS info with past outcomes, partnership sectors, professional English content, downloadable resources
4. **Omar — Government Liaison (45-60):** Needs facts over flash, institutional structure details, large readable typography, structured layouts

**Shared needs:** Credibility, Clarity, Easy contact access, Bilingual support
